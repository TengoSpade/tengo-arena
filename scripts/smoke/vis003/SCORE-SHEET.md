## Marking (after all fights, with the key)

One mark per question: the four role questions, and one per down **in the key**.

- **Tank, healer:** right = the fighter (or the class) that holds the role on that side. A hybrid holds two roles. A wrong fighter, or a right one together with a wrong one, is wrong. "None" is right only when the key says the side has none.
- **Down:** right = the observer's list has the victim and names the one fighter credited with the down. The ability is a bonus, not required. Wrong: several enemies named for one down (assists included), the victim only, "unclear", "unknown", blank, or the down not listed. A down with no fighter behind it (status or zone) is right if the observer says so.
- "Unclear" and "unknown" are wrong everywhere.
