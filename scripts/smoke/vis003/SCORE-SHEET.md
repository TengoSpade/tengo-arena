## Scoring rules

- **Tank, healer:** right = the fighter (or the class) that holds the role on that side. A hybrid holds two roles. A wrong fighter, or a right one together with a wrong one, is wrong. "None" is right only when the key says the side has none.
- **Down:** right = the fighter that struck the last blow. The ability is a bonus, not required. The victim alone, several enemies, "unclear" or blank is wrong. A down with no fighter behind it (status or zone) is right if the observer says so.
