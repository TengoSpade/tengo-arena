**Primary loop in one sentence:** read the week's digest, make the roster and money decisions it calls for, lock a lineup of five with their presets (later their own rules), see the match play out, learn from the report, and advance the week.

**Why the player wants another cycle:** every week has a fixture, against a known rival. The top-4 line and the 8th-place line of the wing table are always in play. Fighters are always mid-arc: a rookie close to a breakout, a veteran near decline, a contract about to expire, a trait about to fade or be earned. Each season end resolves several of these stories at once and opens new ones (rookies, a new tier, a new wing draw).

**How the loop changes with mastery:** M1 mastery is choosing classes, presets, rows and captains that cover each other, reading reports for counters, sharing rounds so that prospects develop, and pricing contracts against the wage budget. Later mastery adds watching and reading a fight (M2), calling at the right moment (after M2, before M3), writing rule lists that exploit an opponent's style (M3), finding undervalued fighters through scouting (M4) and timing academy terms with aging curves (M5). Routine weeks get faster as the player trusts the defaults.

**Unsuccessful paths (M1):**

- **Losing matches** costs points, form-driven gate income and sponsor win bonuses, and moves the club down the wing table. Finishing 5th or lower in the wing means no Reckoning. Finishing 8th in the wing relegates the club (tier 3 clubs cannot be relegated). Relegation cuts the roster cap by 2 (O2) and softens the landing with a −20% drop clause on wages, parachute money and payout-free cap-relief releases (D-C).
- **Benching a prospect** slows his development (M1.1: a fit fighter who starts no round earns ⅓ XP, O17; in M1 he earned none, D-A). The lineup screen shows his level, XP bar and "next level in ≈ N matches", so the cost is visible before the match.
- **Injuries** remove fighters for weeks, or rarely leave a permanent scar (P-11). Only a fighter shown as At risk can suffer a career-ending injury (open-031). If fewer than 5 fit fighters remain, hired blades fill the lineup (open-006).
- **Running out of money** brings Watch, then an embargo, then a Registry advance against next season's grant (D-C). Matches are always played. §6 gives the full ladder.
- **Quitting** at any management screen resumes from the latest save (§3, open-016). Quitting without saving loses only the progress since the last autosave, which is at most one week, or one round during a match (the mid-match slot, O25, D-AD).
- **Finishing the available content** never happens: seasons repeat with new rookies, aging, wing draws and tier movement (P-10). The career ends only when the player abandons it, or from M6 when a dismissed manager declines every job offer (open-007).

**Week processing** (a transient step, not a state; DELEGATED, D-C, D-O): Play match starts it in a week with an own fixture, and Continue in a week without one. From M1.1 it waits inside the player's match at each between-round pause (flow-state-010) and continues when the match is done; Freshness rest and level-up checks are part of it (03 sys-calendar). The weekly autosave (slots auto-1..3) is written when the whole week is committed; a match in progress is saved to its own `auto-round` slot after every round (O25, D-AD). In season 1, P1 first lets AI clubs sign their trials rookies (D-P). **Replay availability (M2; the rule is REQUIRED as O34 (Q56); the technical test and the fallback for older results are the architect's, 05 §9.4).** A replay re-simulates a match's stored inputs, so it needs the match's inputs and round records and the same sim as the one that played it.
- **Live:** the player's own match in progress (Watch); during it the player may rewind within the current round but never look ahead.
- **Replays:** the player's matches of this season and the last, while the sim is unchanged; other clubs' matches for 3 weeks after they are played, after which the compaction of O24 leaves only a summary. A match older than last season is not kept in full.
- **After an update:** once a game update changes a balance value (anything the fight reads: combat constants, class and ability values, ranks, perks, presets and the rule vocabulary, race combat modifiers, the trait effects the sim applies, the captaincy grades and the acting-captain penalty, the Freshness labels), earlier matches cannot be replayed and their reports stay as they are. An update that changes only names, clubs, the economy or the calendar leaves replays working. A match played in M1 (an older engine) has no replay, and a match played before M2 falls back to comparing the whole content hash, so any content edit ends its replay.
- **Telling the player:** a Replay button that cannot work is disabled and says why (EXAMPLE wording: "Older matches between other clubs keep only their summary, to keep saves small"; "Played before a balance update: the replay cannot be rebuilt. The report is unchanged"; "Your match is in progress"); the wording is the UI designer's (open-037).
- **As built (v0.9, PERMITTED-CHOICE):** the disabled Replay button gives one of three reasons: the match's details were compacted ("Older matches between other clubs keep only their summary, to keep saves small."; this also covers a match older than last season), it was played before a balance update ("Played before a balance update: the replay cannot be rebuilt. The report is unchanged."), or it is the player's match in progress ("Your match is in progress."). A replay is checked against the stored round checksum when it loads, and a mismatch is a defect and is never shown. Replays end when what the fight reads changes (the sim hash), and not on a renamed club or a tuned wage (03 sys-match-report; 05 §9.4).

## 6. Failure, recovery, and player commitment

**Status:** REQUIRED where IDs are cited; DELEGATED (D-C) for the money ladder; OPEN working assumptions as tagged.

- **What can be lost:** matches, points, placings and tier; money; development (a fighter who does not play develops at a third of the pace, O17); fighters through injuries (P-11), retirement (D-T: when the rating falls from its peak and with age past the physical peak, at the latest at human-age 58), contract expiry, or from M6 sale; in hardcore, death (P-11, M7). From M6, the manager's job.
- **What persists:** the career, every committed result, fighters' histories and records. Saves restore exactly what was saved (R7, open-016).
- **Recovery path:** a bad season costs a tier and money, but never the ability to play. Fixtures always happen. Short-handed lineups are filled with hired blades (open-006). Free agents at the tier-minimum wage are always available, because the pool is topped up to its floors (D-H). Rookies arrive every season. Grants and sponsor money arrive every season (O10, D-C).
- **Running out of money** (DELEGATED, D-C):
  1. **Watch:** a warning when the balance, plus the next 4 weeks' scheduled income minus wages and costs, would go below 0.
  2. **Embargo**, from any week processing that ends below 0: no offer or renewal above the tier-minimum wage; free-agent offers only at the tier minimum, for 1 season, with no signing cost, and only while the club has fewer than 5 fit fighters; no trials signings; a banner on every hub screen. It lifts at the first processing that ends at 0 or above.
  3. **Registry advance** after 8 consecutive processings below 0: the balance returns to 0, the debt is deducted from later grants (at most 30% of each), and prestige falls by 5.
  4. From M6, debt and missed directives also lower patron patience, which can lead to dismissal and job offers.

  Clubs never fold. AI clubs follow the same ladder (P-04).
- **Retreat / abandonment:** there is no forfeit in M1. After M2 (the calls package, O34 (Q53)), a retreat call may concede a round (P-01; PROPOSED, open-038). The player may end a career from the menu, and the save remains loadable.
- **Retry behavior:** none for committed results (P-05). Loading an earlier save and replaying with identical inputs reproduces the same results (open-016). From M2 replays and from M3 the sandbox allow experiments that never change saved results (P-13).
- **Respec / reversal:** presets, captain, vice-captain, rows and training emphasis can change freely between matches; a captain change only costs something across a lineup lock. From M3, rules are freely editable outside fights. Contracts are binding, and release costs a payout. Academy terms are fixed for the season, and a call-up cannot be reversed (O3).
- **Unwinnable or exhausted state:** none exists. Every season brings grants, rookies and a fresh wing draw, and the hired-blade rule and the journeyman top-up guarantee a playable lineup. From M6, a dismissed manager always receives at least one offer (open-007).
- **Difficulty / assistance:** M1 has one default difficulty. From M7: sliders for AI quality, economy and scouting accuracy, capped catch-up worth less than winning (P-14), and an opt-in hardcore mode (P-11).

## 7. Learning, information, and pacing

**Status:** REQUIRED where IDs are cited; PROPOSED or OPEN as tagged.

- **What a new player must understand first:**
  - Five fighters fight automatically, and each class has a role (Tank, Melee DPS, Ranged DPS, Controller or Healer; O8).
  - Each fighter follows a preset (D-N), and the captain steadies the lineup (O6).
  - The top 4 of each wing reach the Reckoning, and the 8th is relegated (O9, D-C).
  - Money comes as a season budget, and every week costs wages (O10).
  - Fighters who play get better fastest; from M1.1 the bench still earns a third (O17), and tired fighters (Freshness below 60) play worse, so resting matters (O19); the Squad table shows what a tired fighter's rating is worth at his Freshness (O28).
- **How the game teaches them:**
  - Short contextual hints appear at the first occurrence of each screen, dismissible globally.
  - Every preset has a plain-language summary, for example "Guards the most-threatened ally, then fights the nearest enemy".
  - The first match report highlights one cause of the result.
  - M1.1: the first between-round pause and the first level-up card each show a one-time hint (Freshness and swaps; how offers work).
  - The offer dialog shows the wage room after signing and the signing cost. The lineup screen shows injury odds, level and XP bars, Freshness and chemistry. Validation explains itself inline (O13).
- **How tactics are introduced across milestones (P-07, P-22):**
  - *M1:* choose among each class's 2–3 presets, and read in reports which preset rules fired and which never did.
  - *M2:* watch rule badges live and pause to see why a rule fired or was skipped; *after M2:* spend a call.
  - *M3:* open a preset as an editable starting point; make a first edit between rounds when the recap points to a rule that never fired; optional tactics challenges teach conditions, selectors, Only and Prefer, cooldowns and ordering; lint flags mistakes.
- **What remains hidden and why:**
  - In M1, exact stats, potential, pace and traits are visible (open-026).
  - Hidden in every milestone: round seeds (P-05) and AI clubs' private intentions, including the amounts of rival offers (only their count is shown).
  - From M4: true stats, potential and traits sit behind knowledge (R3, O4, P-12); opponents' rules are revealed only by scouting, beyond the style tag and the last lineup (P-13); there is never an outcome forecast.
  - Always available: own contracts, the ledger, standings, fixtures, the cause of every recorded event, and the odds of every risk the player chooses (injury, retirement). Injury odds before a match are estimates from typical rounds and are always shown with ≈ (03 sys-injury).
- **When complexity is introduced:** M1 starts with roster, presets and money. Scouting, the academy, cups and transfers arrive with their milestones. Within a career, the first preseason shows the core actions, and the digest introduces further screens when they first become relevant (document 04).
- **Pressure and downtime:** management has no wall-clock pressure. Pressure comes from the calendar: offers answered each week, contracts expiring at season end, the top-4 and 8th-place lines. From M2, fights run in real time, with free pause and auto-pause (P-01). **Auto-pause defaults (O34 (Q58)):** on when one of the player's fighters is downed, when the player's captain goes down and when overtime starts; off for an enemy downed and for a fighter of the player's club below 25% HP; each is a switch in Settings › Match day › Watching (and a live round also pauses when the window is hidden, on by default). From M2 the Streamlined preset simulates the player's match at Continue and does not open the arena unless the player presses Watch. **Pacing presets** (DELEGATED, D-L, in M1): Thorough stops for every lineup and report; Balanced (default) stops at every lineup and opens the report only after a loss or an injury; Streamlined plays on with the current valid lineup and stops only for blockers, injuries in the lineup, answered offers, contract deadlines and season events; Custom chooses per stop reason. A preset never changes a result. M1.1 adds two Match day settings next to the preset: the between-round pause (Always, the default, O25, D-AE; When urgent; Never) and level-up pacing (Detailed, the default; Balanced; Fast).
- **Pause / stopping points:** any management screen, especially after a match report and at season end.
- **Returning after an absence:** Continue lands on the hub with a "since you were away" panel: the last 3 results, the next fixture, wing position, pending offers and decisions, and budget status.

Document 04 owns exact presentation; document 03 owns hidden versus visible mechanical information.
