# core/game

**Owner:** WP5 (game loop, integrator). **Status:** implemented (M1.2/M1.3). Every function of the phase-B contract is implemented; extra exports are additive. Integrated with the real `fighters` (WP2), `league` (WP3) and `economy` (WP4); `sim`/`tactics` (WP1) run through the same ports as soon as they merge.

## Responsibility
The season state machine and the only entry points that change `GameState`: `createWorld`, `applyCommand`, `advanceWeek`, `runSeasonEnd`. Plus match preparation and read-only views (`getBlockers`, `prepareMatchInput`, `buildMatchReport`, `roster`, `fighterView`, `freeAgents`, `suggestLineup`, `releasePreview`, `captaincyPreview`).

## Pipelines (as implemented)
- **Week** (`leagues.json calendar.weekProcessingOrder`): 1 `fixturesForWeek(…, content)` → `fighters.lineupLock` per playing club (tier, id) → `economy.assignHiredBlades` → locked lineups (eligible entries + blades, written to `club.lineup`) → `sim.simulateMatch` (record `none`) per fixture; 2 `league.recordResult`; 3 `fighters.applyMatchLifecycle` per result (+ one `match.result` news item), `fighters.weeklyLifecycle`; 4–6 `economy.weeklyIncome`, `weeklyCosts`, `financialStatus`; 7 (P1) `releaseTrialsRookies`; 8 `resolveFreeAgentOffers`; tidy lineups/captaincy; 9 AI phase (`ai.planWeek` → `applyCommand` rules, clubs by tier then id, the player's club skipped); 10 `topUpFreeAgentPool`; 11 after `lastRegularWeekIndex` `seedReckonings`, then `nextCalendar`; 12 `league.compactAgedResults` (matches between other clubs that are `leagues.json matchRecords.compactAiMatchesAfterWeeks` weeks old lose their detail; KI-2).
- **Season end**: `promotionRelegation` → `archiveSeason` (pushed to history) → `seasonPrizes` → `applyTierMoves` → `updatePrestige` → club season rows (`leagueSeasonColumns` + money + prestige) → `seasonEndDevelopment` → `seasonEndRetirements` → `contractExpiries` → tidy → `offseasonRecovery` → `poolMaintenance` (leavers archived as `left-unsigned`) → `setupSeason(season+1)` → `seasonStartCalendar` → `generateRookies` → `nextSeasonFinanceSetup` → AI memory reset, news pruned to two seasons, `announceFinalSeasons` → AI phase (`ai.planSeasonEnd`) → retention: `compactHistory`, then departed fighters (retired, career-ended, left unsigned, past hired blades) move to `alumni` and every roster-side reference to them is dropped.
- **New game**: clubs → world-gen fighters joined to their club without contracts → free agents → `setupSeason(1)` → `generateRookies` → `economy.initialFinances` (first economy call: greed, contracts, finances, 95% trim) → `assignTrialsRookies` → per club `ai.planRoster` through commands → `announceFinalSeasons`.

## Decisions (proposed `tech-` entries)
1. **Ports seam.** The engine calls other modules only through `GamePorts`; tests spy on it (pipeline order) or substitute stand-ins. Public functions are bound to the real modules.
2. **Headless mode.** With `playerClubId === null` every club is AI-managed and `advanceWeek` skips the player blockers (soak). `getBlockers` still reports `club.not-chosen` so the UI never offers Continue before a club is chosen.
3. **Season-1 AI opening phase.** Season 1 has no preceding season end, so the AI's season-start phase (`planSeasonEnd`: trials signings, opening offers, captains) runs at the start of the first P1 processing. A player who picks a club starts with no AI-made offers or signings.
4. **All matches inside week processing.** M1 has no separate "play own match" call: `advanceWeek` plays the player's fixture with the rest and `WeekReport.playerFixtureId` names it (05 §5).
5. **Locked lineups.** At lock, ineligible entries drop out, hired blades fill short sides and the locked lineup is written to `club.lineup`; blades and departed fighters are tidied out after the match. A last-resort fill from fit roster fighters protects AI clubs; the player is held by `getBlockers`.
6. **Aftermath persistence.** XP and injuries of a match are not part of `MatchResult`, so a club-scoped `match.result` news item per club and fixture (in the club's own perspective, matching the UI digest template) stores them (`x:<fighterId>`, `i:<fighterId>`); `buildMatchReport` reads it. Gold comes from the ledger (`fixtureId`).
7. **Report timeline** is re-simulated at `summary` only when the result can be replayed on this build (M2: `replayBlocker`, the rule of `replayAvailability`: not compacted, same engine version, same sim content by `simHash` or, without one, the same content hash), and only if every round checksum reproduces.
8. **World rosters in the tier bands.** World-gen fighters get `FighterSpec.ratingTarget` drawn in `leagues.json tiers.ratingBand` (mean of two uniform draws, `STREAM.world`). Without it the potential shifts alone gave every tier a mean rating of about 47 and Muster bills of about 2× WB, so 30% of clubs were insolvent after season 1 (`WORLDGEN_RATING_IN_TIER_BAND`; remove when generation is retuned).
9. **Errors.** Expected failures return stable codes (below); a module exception during a transition becomes `engine.failure` (or `lineup.unfillable`) and no state is returned.
10. **Views without content.** `roster` and `freeAgents` gained an optional `content` parameter (additive) to sort by rating / market wage; without it they sort by id.
11. **Transitions share write-once state.** Each transition starts with `beginTransition` (core/model): mutable parts are deep-copied, `results`, `history` and `alumni` are shared (deep-frozen entries), and the draft is indexed (rosters, the free-agent pool, results per club, fixtures per week). Each transition runs inside `fighters.withDerivedMemo`, so ratings and potentials are computed once per lifecycle epoch.
12. **Captaincy preview.** `captaincyPreview(state, clubId, captainId, viceCaptainId, content)` gives the proposed captain and vice with CQ and grade, who is Snubbed or Passed Over at the next lock (against the captain at the last lock, so switching back is free) and lineup chemistry of the current lineup before and after.
13. **News read state.** `news.setRead { newsIds, read }` sets or clears the flag (the UI's Undo); it is allowed in the season-end phase. `news.markRead` stays as the read-only form.

## Orchestrator decisions (M1 core fix round, pending owner confirmation)
- **Inherited names** never resolve as clubs or fighters: command validation (`clubCheck`, shortlist, release) and views use `getOwn`.
- **D-P, season-1 start.** World creation makes a season-1 Trials Day class of exactly one rookie per club with the normal rookie generator (`fighters.generateRookies(draft, content, count)`); later seasons use the intake formula. The AI season-start setup (captains, vice, training emphasis, opening offers) runs for every club before P1, planned on a scratch copy where each club has already signed its trials rookie at his Trials Day ask, so the offers leave cap and wage room for him. Trials signings wait for the first P1 processing (AI clubs only). `club.choose` withdraws the AI's offers made for the chosen club, so the player makes its market decisions, as after every season end. Starting balance rule: the opening balance is the starting balance (pre-setup cash); the first central-grant installment is one separate setup ledger credit; the season budget counts the season's grant once, and the WB reserve term uses the pre-credit balance, as at every later setup.
- **D-S, auto-captain at kickoff** (`autoCaptain`, week.ts): a club whose captain is missing (none named, or off the roster) gets the vice if fit, else the fit starter with the highest CQ (ties: matchweeks at the club, rating, id), with a `captain.interim` news item (`params.reason` `vice` | `starter`). Same rule for AI clubs. The `captain.missing` blocker is no longer emitted. A captain who is only unavailable keeps the job; the fight lead is then the vice, else the best-CQ starter (fighters.lineupCaptain).

## Match report data (QA round: reserves, entrants, bench XP, compaction)
All additive; `buildMatchReport` still returns `DetailedMatchReport` (= `MatchReport` + `ruleDetails` + `timeline`). A screen that wants the whole match reads these instead of the round-1 five (`ReportSide.fighterIds`, unchanged):
- **`MatchReport.fighters`** has one `FighterRecap` for every fighter who started a round (starters who were swapped out, reserves who came on, emergency entrants), as before; `FighterRecap.role` (`'starter' | 'reserve' | 'emergency'`) and `FighterRecap.roundsStarted` (`number[]`, 1-based; M1.1 results only) are new.
- **`ReportSide.appearedIds`** (per side): everyone who started a round, round-1 five in slot order first, then entrants in the order they came on; each has a line in `fighters`. **`ReportSide.unusedReserveIds`**: squad reserves who never started (they earn bench XP).
- **`MatchReport.totals`** `{ home, away }: ReportTotals` = the sums over `appearedIds` of the lines: `downsInflicted`, `downsSuffered`, `damageDealt`, `healingDone`, `damageTaken?`, `shieldingDone?`, `xp` (round XP, `aftermath.xp`), `benchXp` (sum of `bench`). Downs inflicted by one side equal downs suffered by the other. (QA saw "8 downs" against 10 inflicted by summing only the round-1 five.)
- **`MatchReport.bench`** `BenchXpLine[]` = `{ fighterId, clubId, role: 'reserve' | 'bench', xp }`: the ⅓ bench XP of every fit non-starter of both clubs, home club first, unused squad reserves before the rest; **`aftermath.benchXp`** is the same as a map. Both are absent for matches that did not record it (played by an earlier build, news older than two seasons). Stored as one `bx` param (`"<fighterId>=<xp>,..."`, id order) on each club's `match.result` news item, next to `x:<fighterId>` (round XP of a fighter who started a round) and `i:<fighterId>`.
- **Compacted results** (`MatchReport.compacted: true`; since KI-2 also a match between other clubs a few weeks old, not only last season's): `fighters` carries lines rebuilt from `MatchResult.fighterTotals`, with the club and role of every fighter; `stats` holds `roundsPlayed`, `downsInflicted`, `downsSuffered`, `damageDealt`, `healingDone` and 0 / empty for the rest; `totals` omits `damageTaken` and `shieldingDone`; `timeline` is null and `timelineAvailable` false. `timelineOf` finds an emergency entrant's side from the swap lines or round lineups, not from `fighterStats`, so results compacted by an earlier build (lineups kept, stats emptied) attribute him correctly too.

## M1.1: round-by-round matches (WP-C; docs/design/_drafts/05-technical-m1.1.md)
- **Flow.** `orchestrator.ts`: `startMatch` (squads from the locked `Club.lineup`: 5 starters + 0-2 fit reserves; fight inputs of all seven at the lock with the starters' chemistry) → `playRound` (`simulateRound(deriveSeed(seed, 'round', n))`, then the lifecycle hooks `freshnessAfterRound`, `xpAfterRound`, `injuriesAfterRound`; the hooks see the MatchState with the round appended) → `betweenRounds` / `autoSwapDecisions` → `applyBetweenRound` (legality `checkSwaps`, home then away, forced replacements, emergency entrants, acting captain, `freshnessBetweenRounds`) → `finishMatch`. `playMatchAuto` runs a match with auto decisions on both sides; `roundInputOf` (rounds.ts) rebuilds any round's input from the record.
- **Week split** (`week.ts processWeekLive`): lock every squad, play the player's match first round by round; at a between-round step `advance` pauses per `Club.pauseBetweenRounds` (`always` / `when_urgent` = `BetweenRoundView.urgent` / `never`) with `GameState.liveMatch` set and returns the screen; the next `advance` takes the player's `match.swap` decisions (else the suggestions) and plays on. When his match is over the other fixtures are played (auto), then steps 2-11 as before; step 3 is `matchEndProgression` per match (plus auto-resolution of policy level-ups still pending). `advanceWeek` is the same without pauses (it also finishes a live match). Pausing and accepting every suggestion gives the same state as an unpaused week (tested over a season).
- **While a match is live** only `match.swap`, `match.watch` (M2), `news.*`, `shortlist.toggle`, `levelup.policy`, `autoswap.set`, `pacing.set` are accepted (`match.in-progress`; `levelup.choose` → `levelup.match-in-progress`); `getBlockers` is empty.
- **Auto-swap** (combat sys-match): forced first (best-fit reserve: same role family, rating, Freshness, squad index); threshold = `freshnessBelow` (+ `lostRoundBonus` if the side lost the round just played), **lowest Freshness first** (sw-007 settled as §A4 says), reserve Freshness ≥ starter + margin and rating ≥ starter − gap; `keepOneForInjury` keeps the last swap until the last possible step (after round bestOf − 1). Freshness compared in whole points.
- **Acting captain** at each step: the club captain if active; else the acting captain if still active and he is the vice or the vice is not active; else the vice if active; else the highest lock CQ (ties lower slot). All but the club captain lead at CQ − `actingCaptainCqPenalty`; the lock lead keeps his lock value.
- **Emergency entrant** (no swap left): best fit roster fighter outside the squad (family, rating, Freshness, id), else a one-week hired blade of the injured fighter's class (economy's blade terms).
- **Records.** `MatchResult.reserveInputs` holds the reserves who entered and the emergency entrants (what replay needs). Swap reasons: `injury` when the outgoing fighter is injured, `player` for the player's side when he sent `match.swap`, else `auto`; `emergency` with `costsSwap: false`.
- **Report.** `DetailedMatchReport.timeline` (null for M1 results): squads, per-round lineups (rows, acting captain, Freshness at the round start, entered flags), steps (swap lines, injured, acting captains, Freshness after) and per-fighter rounds / entered after / withdrawn after / injured in. M1.1 results replay round by round for the key events.
- **Views.** `advance`, `betweenRoundView`, `levelUpView` (pending offers oldest first; stat and rating after a stat pick on a copy with `train100 + gain`; legality; the auto-pick), `suggestLineup` returns `squad.set` with reserves. `suggestLineup` is `ai.chooseLineup` (no second rotation rule): the best five by role and rating, then the AI Freshness rotation (`levels.json aiRotation`): a starter projected Tired at his next round is replaced by the best rested fighter of his role family within the rating gap (most tired first, at most 3), a starter below the spent floor (30) by any fit fighter at 40 or more, and the two reserves are rested fighters (Freshness at or above the threshold; nobody Tired is named, because unused reserves rest less). In a Reckoning week the Tired step uses `tournamentRestBelow`. A starter between the spent floor and 40 (labelled Spent) with no comparable rested fighter of his role family (the lone Tank, say) stays. `auto-pick.test.ts` pins this.
- **Blind swaps.** `betweenRounds` / `betweenRoundView` / `AdvanceOutcome.pause` give the player the screen with his own side's auto-swap suggestions only: the opponent side's `suggestions` is `[]` (its pending decision is not even computed for the view; it is still taken by the AI's rules when the step is applied). With no player club (headless, spectated) both sides carry them. `match.swap` is accepted only from the player's club (`state.playerClubId`); any other club, the opponent included, is refused with `match.not-player-club` (checked before the match state, so nothing about the live match leaks either).
- **Errors (new).** `match.in-progress`, `match.not-live`, `match.not-between-rounds`, `match.not-player-club`, `match.not-a-side`, `swap.<SwapRejection>`, `squad.reserves-size`, `squad.duplicate`, `squad.not-on-roster`, `squad.reserve-unfit`, `levelup.match-in-progress`, `levelup.unknown`, `levelup.mode`, `autoswap.rules`, `pacing.level-up`, `pacing.pause`; M2: `watch.not-shown`, `watch.mismatch`, `match.not-watching`, `watch.round`, `watch.tick`, `watch.mode`, `replay.unavailable`, `replay.no-round`, `replay.mismatch`, `inspect.tick` (see M2 below).

## R2 box score (T001): per fighter, per match and per round
All additive and optional; nothing here is an event, so no event hash and no golden moved. `MatchReport` (`buildMatchReport`) now carries, for every fighter who started a round (starters, swapped-in reserves, emergency entrants; not on a compacted report):
- **`FighterRecap.box?: FighterBox`** = `BoxScoreLine` (`damageDone`, `damageReceived`, `healingDone`, `healingReceived?`, `shieldingDone`, `downsInflicted`, `downsSuffered`; points) plus `roundsStarted` (count), `freshnessBefore` / `freshnessAfter` (0-100, whole points; `null` when not recorded) and `rounds: FighterBoxRound[]` (one `BoxScoreLine` per round he started, with `round`, `freshnessBefore`, `freshnessAfter`). Damage is HP damage (shield absorption excluded); healing is **effective** (overheal excluded; self-heals count on both ends; Deathward's heal above the 1 HP floor counts); shielding is the shield points granted.
- **`freshnessBefore`** is his Freshness at the start of his first round (a reserve: when he came on); **`freshnessAfter`** is right after the drain of his last round (the between-round rest is not in it).
- **`ReportRound.totals?: { home, away }: BoxScoreLine`** each side's totals for the round (sum of its five fighters' `rounds` lines). **`ReportTotals.healingReceived?`** the match total.
- **Where the numbers come from.** The match totals are the stored `FighterMatchStats` (new optional `healingReceived`, a sim counter next to `healingDone`: `Tally.healingReceived`, summed per round by `mergeStats`, outside the checksum and every event). The per-round lines come from the same re-simulation that rebuilds the key events (`simulateRound` returns each round's `fighterStats`), so they exist only when `MatchReport.timelineAvailable`; otherwise `box.rounds` is `[]` and `ReportRound.totals` is absent. Freshness comes from the round lineups: `RoundSideLineup.freshness` (round start, as before) and the new optional **`RoundSideLineup.freshnessAfter`** (by slot, written by the orchestrator right after `freshnessAfterRound`). A result played before this change has no `freshnessAfter` (null) and no stored `healingReceived`; the report re-derives the latter from the re-simulated rounds when it can and leaves it absent otherwise. Whole-match (M1) results have totals and no Freshness or rounds.
- **Saves.** The two stored fields are optional in `save/schema.ts` and in the model types; older saves load unchanged, so `STATE_SCHEMA_VERSION` is not bumped (same precedent as `roundDamageTaken`).

## M2: watch, playback, replay, resume (WP-M2B; docs/design/_drafts/05-technical-m2.md §A3, §A4)
Implemented, integrated with WP-M2A's sim (log format 2, `inspectRoundAt`, `simContentHash`) on `m2/integration`. Every result records `simHash`; core/sim caches it per ContentDB (55-130 ms the first time), and week processing computes it at its start, never in the middle of a match; `warmReplayHash(content)` lets the UI do it at idle. A probe (`inspectPlayback`) shows the state at the end of the tick and evaluations as of `RoundProbe.evaluatedTick` (tick + 1); at the round's last tick (`durationTicks - 1`) the survivors read `blockedBy: 'round_over'`. `prepareRound` builds a call plan only from `liveMatch.watch.calls`, which only `match.call` writes (refused), and `validateState` refuses a live match holding calls (core/sim would refuse the plan, so it could never play on).

**The commit point of a watched round (owner answer to Q60, option A).** A watched round's lineups, presets and seed are locked and autosaved before it is shown; the round is committed when its playback ends, and nothing of its result is in `GameState` before then.
1. **Play match, Watch:** `advance(state, content, { playerMatch: 'watch' })` locks every squad and starts the player's match (`startMatch`) but plays no round: `liveMatch` is in phase `round_pending` with `watch { mode 'watch', round 1, cursorTick 0, calls [] }`, and `AdvanceOutcome.watch` is `{ fixtureId, round, cursorTick }` (`week` and `pause` null). The UI writes the `auto-round` save and opens the viewer only after it succeeded. The other fixtures of the week are played after the player's match, as before.
2. **Watching:** `liveRoundPlayback(state)` builds the round with `prepareRound` (the builder `playRound` uses) and simulates it at `full` with the round seed; `RoundPlayback.result` is null. Nothing is written while playback runs.
3. **Playback stops** (pause, auto-pause, window hidden): `match.watch { round, tick }` moves `watch.cursorTick` (the reveal frontier: `max(cursor, tick)`, so a stop after a rewind is accepted and the frontier never moves back; at most the time cap), then the UI writes `auto-round`. The cursor changes no input (`m2-contracts.test.ts`).
4. **Round end or "Skip to end of round":** `advance({ watched: { round, logHash } })` with the hash of the log the viewer loaded. `playRound` simulates `prepareRound`'s input at `full`, compares the log hash before writing anything (`watch.mismatch` on a difference, in every build), commits the round with `eventHash` null like every committed round (P-05), runs the round hooks, then the between-round step: a pause per `pauseBetweenRounds` (`watch.round` null), or the auto decisions and the next round waiting to be watched (`watch.round` n + 1, cursor 0). The deciding round finishes the match and the week.
5. **Between rounds → Continue:** `advance()` applies the swaps (the player's `match.swap`, else the suggestions) and stops at the next round waiting to be watched.
6. **"Simulate the rest":** `match.watch { mode: 'simulate' }` (the round on screen, or between rounds the round about to start) sets `watch { mode 'simulate', round null }`; the next `advance()` commits the pending round from its locked inputs without a hash and simulates the rest with the M1.1 pauses. Watch never comes back within the match.
7. **Quit and resume:** the last `auto-round` holds the locked round and the cursor of the last stop. Loading it, `liveRoundPlayback` gives the same log, `startTick` = the cursor; the commit is the same as without the quit (the outcome is fixed by the inputs, P-05). A quit while playing resumes at the last stop (A17 item 14).

**Refusals (no state).** `watch.not-shown` (details `fixtureId`, `round`): an `advance` without a shown log while a round waits to be watched, or `advanceWeek` on a watched match (it cannot stop at a round); the UI routes it to `#/arena`. `watch.mismatch` (details `reason`): `'log'` when the hash is not the round's log, `'round'` when `watched` names another round or no watched round is waiting (a repeated or stale finish never plays on or starts a week).

**Commands.** `match.watch` is in `LIVE_MATCH_ALLOWED`. Refusals: `match.not-player-club`, `match.not-live`, `match.not-watching` (a simulated match; a cursor between rounds; `mode: 'watch'` after simulating), `watch.round`, `watch.tick` (not an integer from 0 to the time cap), `watch.mode`. `match.call` stays refused (`match.calls-unavailable`).

**Playback views** (`playback.ts`; pure reads, every log from `simulateRound` at `full`):
- `liveRoundPlayback(state)`: errors `match.not-live`, `match.not-watching`.
- `roundPlayback(state, fixtureId, round)`: a committed round of a stored result (`roundInputOf`) or of the live match's earlier rounds (`liveRoundInputOf`), verified against the stored checksum. Errors `replay.unavailable` (details `reason`), `replay.no-round`, `replay.mismatch` (a defect, never shown).
- `matchPlayback(state, fixtureId)`: rounds and swap lines without simulating; live, the round being watched is listed with winner and duration null.
- `replayAvailability(state, fixtureId)`: `live`, `not-played`, `compacted` (O24; or a fixture of a season older than the previous one, dropped with its results), `other-build`, else `{ available, rounds }`. The rule is `replayBlocker` (`rounds.ts`): compacted; engine version; then `simHash` against `ports.sim.simContentHash`, and for results without a `simHash` (or a sim that cannot name one) the career content hash. `buildMatchReport` re-simulates its timeline under the same rule, so a non-sim content edit keeps the report's box-score lines once results carry a `simHash`.
- `inspectPlayback(state, fixtureId, round, tick)`: the round's exact input to `inspectRoundAt`; `inspect.tick` past the cursor (live) or past the round's last tick (`durationTicks - 1`).

**Records.** `finishMatch` writes `MatchResult.simHash` when `ports.sim.simContentHash` names one. `prepareRound` puts the round's call plans (RESERVED: `liveMatch.watch.calls`, always empty before the calls package) in `RoundInput.calls` and `RoundLineups.calls`; `playRound` clears `watch.calls` once the round is committed, so no plan carries into a later round (also after "Simulate the rest"); `roundInputOf` copies them back. AI matches never watch: only the player's fixture can start in Watch, and `playMatchAuto` plays the others.

**Ports.** `GamePorts.sim` gains the optional `simContentHash` (null while core/sim's is a stub; cached per ContentDB) and `inspectRoundAt`.
